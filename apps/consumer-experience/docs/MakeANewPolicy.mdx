# Create a new Policy

To replicate the user experience, DO NOT use your regular Zinnia Live user email (e.g. your `first.last@zinnia.com` email). This will result in odd experiences because of roles and permissions conflicts.

<strong>
  If this is your first time logging into MPV OR you are replicating a specific
  user experience use new email + ssn
</strong>
- email should be an alias of your AD email in the form of
`first.last+fakeSSN-extra-descriptor@zinnia.com` - Generate a random 9 digit
number to use as a GovtId for that email address

## Creating a policy in test harness

<ol style={{ listStyle: 'auto' }}>
<li>Go [here](https://qa-zahara-ui.zinnia.io/#/app/policy)<br/><strong>Note:</strong> If you don't see the `Add policy` button, you need to
request access to add policies through Conductor1 [see
here](https://zinnia.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ZAHARA/pages/5231738943/Requesting+Access+to+Zahara+UI+via+ConductorOne)</li>
  <li>
    In the upper right corner, download the XML of the product type you want to
    create OR go to a policy you are replicating and download from the policy
    page
  </li>
  <li>
    click on the info icon to find out what fields you need to replace in the
    downloaded xml
    <ul>
    Hints:
    <li>for annuity, update `party_annuitant_1`</li>
    <li>FOR ANNUITY PRODUCTS: you will need to remember what date you set this to for a later step</li>
    <li>if you are testing free look, make sure that issue date is within the last 30 days</li>

    </ul>

  </li>
  <li>
    Search for newly added item (use last name or policy number) this may take
    up to 30 seconds, you can just keep clicking "REFRESH" button
  </li>
  <li>Click on the new item to lifecycle (this will convert it to active)</li>
</ol>

### Lifecycle after policy is created

> this simulates the end of day processing for policies

#### For Life Policy

1. click `LIFECYCLE`

#### For Fixed Annuity

<ol style={{ listStyle: 'auto' }}>
  <li>
    go to `TRANSACTIONS` tab
    <ol style={{ listStyle: 'auto' }}>
      <li>click on the plus sign in upper right corner</li>
      <li>change type to initial premium</li>
      <li>change effective date to match the issue date</li>
      <li>change party to OWNER</li>
      <li>update request amount to something pretty high</li>
      <li>click "submit"</li>
    </ol>
    <li>Click `LIFECYCLE`</li>
  </li>
</ol>
